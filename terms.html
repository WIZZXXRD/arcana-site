Entertainment & personal reflection

Arcana provides symbolic Tarot, horoscopes, compatibility, numerology and reflections for entertainment and personal exploration. It does not guarantee predictions or provide professional medical, financial or legal advice. Compatibility and numerology are symbolic conventions, not scientific assessments. Lunar calculations are approximate, not a precise astronomical ephemeris.

Content is bundled and composed locally, without a paid AI interpretation API. Decisions about your life remain yours; consult an appropriately qualified professional where needed.

Accounts & local records

Google sign-in is required. A restored, unexpired signed session permits free offline use after online authentication, for up to 30 days. Key/storage loss, reinstall, expiry or online revocation can require sign-in again. Premium has a separate online verification requirement.

Switching accounts isolates local stores. Sign-out retains Journal records for the same account on the same device. Uninstalling or clearing storage can lose them. No cloud Journal sync, reinstall or new-device Journal recovery is provided. Saved text remains in its original language.

Free access, rewards & Premium

Free previews and daily reflections require authentication. Selected details can be unlocked through optional rewarded ads after genuinely earning the disclosed reward. Rewards apply only to the stated scope: a Journal detail grant is for one visit; a Library reward lasts for the current app process. Rewards are not money, purchased credits, permanent rights or transferable entitlements.

Verified Premium opens current protected details and Tarot Library without rewarded ads. Pending or ineligible purchases do not grant access. Verification is cached in memory for at most 15 minutes or subscription expiry; restart or expired verification requires an online check. Network failure cannot extend Premium.

Google Play subscriptions

Premium is intended as an optional monthly auto-renewing Google Play subscription. The actual localized price, billing period and availability must be shown by Play in the app before purchase. This website offers no checkout and promises no trial, introductory offer or fixed price.

Play governs payment, renewal, cancellation, grace periods, holds, pauses, expiry, refunds and revocation. Manage or cancel in Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation normally retains eligible paid access until verified expiry; deletion or uninstalling does not cancel billing. Statutory rights and refund procedures require operator review and are not waived by this draft.

Purchase ownership & restoration

Purchases belong to the authenticated Arcana Google identity, which may differ from the Play billing account. Use the same Arcana account and the app’s Restore action on another installation. The server revalidates retained encrypted purchase identifiers with Play. Email or a token alone cannot transfer ownership.

Restoration depends on production configuration and authoritative ownership checks. Deleted or retention-expired records can require valid Play rediscovery and re-verification. Purchase restoration does not restore a local Journal.

Sign-out, deletion & termination

Android Profile supports sign-out, account switching and confirmed online deletion after recent Google sign-in. Deletion removes live account/indexed sessions/purchase records and that account’s Journal/preferences on the initiating device. It cannot remotely erase other devices, delete your Google identity or cancel Play subscriptions. Temporary security/event and backup records follow the privacy boundaries, pending approval.

Access can end when authentication expires or is revoked, or when a subscription is no longer eligible. Additional operator-initiated suspension/termination rules, acceptable-use procedures, notice and dispute rights are not approved and must be finalized before release. See Account Deletion for the external-flow limitation.
