A few things to know.

Is Arcana available to download?

Not yet. Arcana is coming soon to Google Play. No public release date has been announced.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Google sign-in is required. After online authentication, free local content can work offline while your signed account session remains valid, for up to 30 days. A fresh installation or expired session requires online sign-in. Premium requires separate online verification.

Are readings generated by an online AI service?

No. Interpretations use bundled content and local calculations. Your Journal, questions, birth dates and local profile inputs are not uploaded to Arcana’s account or advertising services. Google authentication, billing, consent and eligible ads involve network processing.

Will my Journal move to a new phone?

No. The Journal is local to the device and excluded from Android backup and device transfer. Signing out keeps records for the same account on that device; uninstalling or clearing app storage can lose them. Purchase restoration does not restore Journal entries.

Can I delete my account?

The Android account flow supports confirmed online deletion after a recent Google sign-in. A secure external web deletion request flow is still pending. See the Account Deletion page for scope, limitations and subscription guidance.
