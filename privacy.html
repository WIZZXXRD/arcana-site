About this policy

This page describes the Phase 7.5 implementation of Arcana: Tarot & Astrology, developed under the brand Wrong Wizard. Production services and their final configuration require separate acceptance. Wrong Wizard is a developer brand; the operator’s legal identity has not been supplied.

The Android app and this website have different data behavior. This static website has no account form, analytics, advertising SDKs or third-party embeds. Its only application storage is your EN/RU language preference in browser local storage, removable through browser site settings. The hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata under its own policies.

Google sign-in & account identifiers

Google authentication is mandatory. There is no guest, password or alternative-provider login. Android Credential Manager obtains a Google ID token and sends it over HTTPS to Arcana’s account service. The service verifies Google’s signature, issuer, audience, expiry, a one-use challenge and device proof.

The account identifier is a namespaced SHA-256 hash derived from Google’s stable subject identifier. Email is display information, not the ownership key. Verified name and email are included in the signed session certificate. Raw Google ID tokens and profile claims are not retained in the server ledger or application logs.

Account sessions & security

The service issues a random session secret and a signed device-bound free-session certificate valid for 30 days. The server retains the secret’s hash, account/device binding and creation/expiry information. Android protects session material with Keystore-backed AES-GCM encryption and excludes it from backup. Private device keys do not leave Keystore.

An unexpired validated session permits free offline content. Expiry, key loss or reinstall requires online sign-in. Revocation cannot be detected immediately offline: free access may continue until certificate expiry. Premium is verified independently online and cached only in memory for at most 15 minutes or subscription expiry, whichever comes first.

Your Journal & local inputs

Readings and calculations use bundled content and local processing, without a paid AI interpretation service. Local profile inputs, birth dates, zodiac sign, daily state, language, theme and reminder settings use account-scoped app-private storage. Journal questions, saved readings, cards and notes use an account-scoped local Room database. Some calculation inputs are held in transient screen state.

Arcana does not upload Journal entries, local profile inputs, birth dates or calculation questions to account or advertising services. Journal storage relies on Android app isolation, without additional database encryption. Session secrets have separate Keystore encryption.

Signing out or switching hides the prior account’s stores; signing back into the same account on the same device can reopen them. There is no cloud Journal sync or recovery after reinstalling or on a new device. Journal, preferences and session material are excluded from Android backup/device transfer. Device compromise, screenshots and manufacturer behavior remain separate risks. Optional reminders are local Android notifications, not cloud messaging.

Billing & encrypted purchase recovery

Google Play processes payments; Arcana receives no payment-card details. Your Play billing account may differ from your Arcana Google account. Purchases include an obfuscated Arcana account identifier.

The service receives purchase token, product, account identifier, device public key, nonce, time and signature to verify ownership, subscription state and expiry with Play before acknowledgement and entitlement signing. Firestore retains account/session indices, token digests, ownership/replacement records and required purchase tokens encrypted with a separate symmetric Cloud KMS key and account/product binding. Raw tokens are decrypted temporarily for fresh verification.

Retained encrypted tokens support restoration for the same verified Arcana account on another installation or device. An email or copied token alone cannot authorize another account. Recovery depends on production configuration, authoritative Play binding and retained or rediscovered purchase records; it is not Journal recovery.

Advertising & consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob rewarded ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent processing. UMP may contact Google before ads load. Ads initialize/load only when UMP permits ad requests and eligibility checks pass. Request eligibility is not proof of consent to personalized ads. Required privacy options are available in the app; changes invalidate ads. Verified Premium prevents new ad requests.

Depending on final SDK configuration, region and consent, Google services may process identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, interactions and diagnostics. Arcana does not request device location permission or add developer analytics, mediation partners or cloud Journal sync. Exact collection/sharing classifications and regional behavior require production review. Local interpretation content does not mean zero SDK network collection.

Sign-out, deletion & other devices

Sign-out immediately clears the local session and attempts online revocation of that server session. Offline sign-out cannot revoke it remotely at once; other devices have independent sessions. Local records remain for later same-account, same-device access.

Confirmed online deletion requires proof-bound Google sign-in within the previous five minutes. It removes the live account index, indexed sessions, purchase ciphertext/ownership/replacement records and associated observations. Delayed purchase notifications do not recreate the account. Temporary pseudonymous replay/rate records and independent event receipts may remain until cleanup.

The initiating Android device clears only that account’s Journal/preferences and cancels reminders. Other devices cannot be remotely wiped: access is rejected online or ends at free-certificate expiry. Later sign-in does not recover deleted Journal or purchase inventory. Deletion does not delete your Google account or cancel Play subscriptions. See Account Deletion.

Third-party services

Configured app services include Google Identity/Credential Manager, Google Play Billing and subscription verification, AdMob/UMP, and Google Cloud Run, Firestore, IAM Credentials and Cloud KMS. Cloud infrastructure can process IP/request metadata and access/audit logs; request-body tracing must remain disabled and access restricted. This website is intended for GitHub Pages hosting.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement. The operator must approve actual processor relationships, hosting regions and international-transfer terms.
