Account Deletion

Understand how deleting your Arcana account affects account records, local Journal entries and Google Play subscriptions.

Web deletion is not yet available

Secure web deletion · integration pending

This public page is informational. The authenticated external deletion request flow has not been implemented or deployed. There is no working request form on this website, and visiting it does not submit a deletion request.

A production flow must connect verified Google authentication to the account-deletion service: secure session handling, recent sign-in proof, CSRF/replay protection, explicit confirmation and truthful result handling. It must never authorize deletion using an email, public account ID or purchase token alone.

Delete through the Android app

The Phase 7.5 Android flow supports these steps once production account services are configured and available. Arcana is not yet publicly available on Google Play.

  1. If you have a subscription, review or cancel it first in Google Play subscriptions.
  2. Open Arcana and sign into the Google account used for that Arcana account.
  3. Open Profile, choose account deletion and review the confirmation.
  4. Connect to the internet and complete Google sign-in again when asked. Deletion requires sign-in within the previous five minutes and device-bound proof.
  5. Confirm deletion. Treat it as complete only when the app confirms the server result. A connection or local-cleanup error must not be treated as successful deletion.

What is deleted

Successful server deletion removes the live account index, all indexed sessions, retained encrypted purchase tokens, ownership/replacement records and associated subscription observations. Delayed subscription notifications cannot recreate the deleted account.

The initiating Android device clears that account’s Journal/preferences and cancels reminders. Individual Journal entries and notes can also be deleted without deleting the account.

What may remain temporarily

Pseudonymous replay/rate records may remain for one day and independent purchase-event deduplication receipts for seven days until cleanup. Firestore TTL cleanup is eventual. Backup and infrastructure-log retention, deletion propagation and operational controls require operator approval; no immediate backup-erasure guarantee is made.

Other devices’ local data cannot be remotely wiped. Sessions are rejected online, but offline free access can continue until the signed certificate expires, up to 30 days. Existing verified Premium memory leases can last at most 15 minutes or subscription expiry. See retention boundaries.

Local device data & later sign-in

Deletion on one device cannot erase another device’s Journal or storage. Manage local data on each device separately. Uninstalling or clearing storage removes local records but is not an authenticated server account-deletion request.

There is no cloud Journal backup or reinstall/new-device recovery. Later Google sign-in can create an empty account index but cannot recover deleted server inventory or Journal entries. Re-enrolling an eligible purchase requires authoritative Play binding.

Deletion does not cancel subscriptions

Deleting Arcana account data, uninstalling or clearing storage does not cancel Play subscriptions and does not delete your Google account.

Open Google Play subscriptions using the Google account that pays for the subscription, select Arcana if listed and use Play’s management/cancellation controls. That account can differ from your Arcana sign-in. Review Play’s displayed renewal and cancellation information.
